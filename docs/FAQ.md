### Common Troubleshooting Tips

1. **Canary Deployment Not Receiving Traffic**: Ensure that your routing rules are correctly configured to divert a portion of traffic to the canary instance.
2. **Metrics Do Not Reflect Expected Improvements**: Verify that the fixes were deployed correctly, and there are no configuration mismatches between canary and production environments.
3. **Unexpected Errors or Performance Degradation**: Roll back the canary deployment immediately and investigate logs and error reports for clues about what went wrong.
4. **Differences Between Local Testing and Canary Results**: Ensure all environmental variables and third-party services are consistent across development, testing, and production environments.

Remember, successful canary testing relies on precise monitoring, quick feedback loops, and the ability to rollback changes at the first sign of trouble.
